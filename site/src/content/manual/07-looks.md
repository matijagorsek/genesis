<p>Day and night are one key: Settings › Day / night, or say "dark mode". The desktop, the terminal and the assistant switch together.</p>
<p>Or describe what you want:</p>
<pre><code>genesis-theme make "foggy morning by the sea"
genesis-theme make "warm autumn library at night"
genesis-theme undo</code></pre>
<p>Twenty seconds later every application, the panel, the terminal and the wallpaper are in a palette made for that, by the model on this machine — and <code>undo</code> is the look before. What it proposes is checked before it reaches the screen, so text always reads. Open terminals keep their colours; new ones take the new ones.</p>
