<p>Nothing, by default. The models run here; the crash you ask about, the screen you ask about, the files you opt in — none of it is uploaded, because there is nowhere for it to go. Genesis makes three kinds of network connection on its own: fetching updates, downloading the model pack you chose, and installing the apps you asked for. That's the list.</p>
<p>The one exception is clearly labelled: <b>Claude Code</b>, with your own account, is one click away in the menu if you want a bigger model for a bigger job. It says so on the button, and it is off until you sign in. Every tool the assistant has is listed under Settings › Tools, and you can add your own.</p>
