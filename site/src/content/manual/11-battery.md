<p>Pull the cable out of a laptop and the big models are put away; every question goes to the small always-loaded one, and the assistant gets shorter rather than quiet. Plug back in and they're allowed to load again. A desktop is left alone. If you want the big model on battery anyway: <code>genesis-power off</code>, once.</p>
