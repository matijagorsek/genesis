<p><kbd>Meta</kbd>+<kbd>Space</kbd>, anywhere. Type what you want in plain words and press Enter.</p>
<ul>
<li>"a checklist app that saves to a file"</li>
<li>"a pomodoro timer with a bell"</li>
<li>"rename the photos in this folder by the date they were taken"</li>
<li>"why is my disk full"</li>
</ul>
<figure><img src="screens/fresh-run/03-pomodoro-made-by-the-2B-model.png" alt="The maker after building a pomodoro timer: steps on the left, preview on the right"><figcaption>The maker, having built a pomodoro timer on a fresh machine with the smallest model. Steps on the left, the thing itself on the right.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Every step shows as it happens; a preview appears when the thing runs. When it's done, <b>Install as app</b> puts it in the menu. The project is a folder under your home, named after what you asked for. To change it, type in the box at the bottom — "make the button bigger", "add a dark mode" — and Enter.</p>
<div class="note"><p><b>A step can take minutes on a machine without a GPU.</b> The line under the steps says how long the current one has run. It is working, not stuck.</p></div>
<p>The same key answers questions. Ask about the machine, a file, a command that failed — the answer comes from the model on this computer, with the permission rules below still in force.</p>
