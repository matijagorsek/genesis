<p>Write the ISO (about 9 GB — it carries the smallest model pack, so the first boot answers offline) to a USB stick of 16 GB or more, boot from it, and the installer asks for a disk, a language and <b>an account</b>. It will not continue without the account — that is deliberate, because a machine with no account has no way to log in.</p>
<div class="note"><p><b>The installer will look stuck. It isn't.</b> After you press Install, the bar sits at "Deployment starting" for <b>10 to 30 minutes</b> while 13 GB is written to the disk. Nothing moves on screen during that time. Two ways to see it working: the disk light on the machine, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>F3</kbd> for a console where <code>top</code> shows the copy running (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>F6</kbd> brings the installer back).</p></div>
