Claude, with your account

Genesis' own maker runs on this machine. If you also want Claude, Genesis includes Claude Code, Anthropic's terminal agent: it signs in with your Claude account in the terminal on first start, no API key. It is a separate app; nothing from the maker goes to it unless you open a project there.

Voice

Speech recognition understands many languages (whisper); the spoken replies are English only for now. Machines with 16 GB or a GPU get the more accurate "small" speech model.

Phone

Pairs over your own network with KDE Connect, encrypted device to device. Share text to Genesis starting with ask and the answer comes back as a notification; the phone also gets commands (day / night, lock, apply the staged update).
