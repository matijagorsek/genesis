Genesis updates itself in the background and applies the update at the next restart. Nothing restarts on its own.

What runs locally, and whether anything else is involved.

Models

Chosen at first run for this hardware. Downloaded once, kept under /var/lib/genesis/models.

How much Genesis may do on its own

The default for new jobs. You can change it per job in the maker.

Day / night

Switches the colours of every app, the panel and the login screen. Also Meta+Shift+T.

Claude, with your account

Genesis' own maker runs on this machine. If you also want Claude, Genesis includes Claude Code, Anthropic's terminal agent: it signs in with your Claude account in the terminal on first start, no API key. It is a separate app; nothing from the maker goes to it unless you open a project there.

Claude Code
…
Opens a terminal in your projects folder; the first start installs it and asks you to sign in.

Voice

Speech recognition understands many languages (whisper); the spoken replies are English only for now. Machines with 16 GB or a GPU get the more accurate "small" speech model.

Files Genesis may search

Opt folders in and Genesis can answer from your own notes, documents and code ("what did I write about the trip?"). The index is a local file; nothing is uploaded. Hidden folders, build trees and files over 20 MB are skipped.

Phone

Pairs over your own network with KDE Connect, encrypted device to device. Share text to Genesis starting with ask and the answer comes back as a notification; the phone also gets commands (day / night, lock, apply the staged update).

Phone app

The Genesis companion app (Android first) shows your jobs, lets you answer permission cards and start a make from the phone, and can fetch one screenshot on demand. It talks to this machine directly over your own network, encrypted, after scanning this code once. No relay, no cloud.

Scan with the Genesis app. Regenerate the code to unpair every phone.

Activity

Every action Genesis was asked to take, and what the permission rules decided. Kept on this machine in a tamper-evident log.

Backup and moving

One file with everything Genesis made and knows about you: the projects with their recipes, your Genesis settings, the folders you opted in for search, the phone pairings, the undo history. No models (they download again), no keys or passwords. Copy it to a new Genesis and restore it there.

Undo history

Every job that changed something outside its project was snapshotted first. Undo from the maker, or with genesis-txd undo <id> in a terminal.
