    Six things worth knowing
    • Meta+Space anywhere: ask a question or say what to make.
    • Meta+Shift+V: say it out loud; it sends when you pause.
    • Meta+Shift+A: drag over anything on screen and ask about it.
    • Meta+Shift+M: how much Genesis may do on its own.
    • Meta+Shift+T: day or night, for the whole desktop.
    • Stop ends a job at once; Undo takes back what it changed.
