Genesis · a Linux desktop with a local AI inside

A computer that can make things for you.

Genesis is a complete desktop operating system: browser, files, office, media, an app store. It also has a maker built in. Describe what you want, and it builds it, right on your machine, with no cloud and no account.

Every change asks you first and can be undone.

Preview renderings from the real Genesis image, version 0.1.27. Real screenshots from the running system follow.

Log in

It looks like a computer, because it is one.

Your own login screen with the Genesis look. No cloud account, no sign-up, no terms to click through.

2 of 6

First start

Genesis checks what your computer can do.

On the first start it looks at your graphics card, memory and disk, and suggests AI models that fit. They download once and then run on your machine only. Nothing you type ever leaves it.

3 of 6

A complete desktop

Everything you expect is already there.

Web browser, files, terminal, documents, photos, music, video, calculator, an app store with thousands of apps, and office, mail and image tools. Genesis is one more entry in the menu, not the whole menu.

4 of 6

Ask for what you want

Press one shortcut and describe it.

Meta+Space opens a small box. Type what you need in plain words: a timer, a tool that renames your photos, a website for your club. Genesis takes it from there.

5 of 6

Watch it get made

Step by step, live, on your machine.

The maker shows each step as it works, and the thing it builds appears next to it while it is still being made. Press Install and it shows up in your app menu like any other program.

6 of 6

You stay in control

Every change asks first, and every change can be undone.

Reading is free. Writing inside your project is allowed. Touching the rest of the system asks you. Installing software or changing settings asks and is recorded, so you can undo it days later. This settings section is the last piece still being built.

Why on your machine?

Because your files, your photos and your ideas should not need to be uploaded anywhere to be useful. Genesis picks AI models that fit the computer you already have, from a small laptop to a gaming PC. Bigger machine, smarter helper. Same rules: it asks, it remembers, it can undo.

Built on Fedora and KDE Plasma. Source and images: github.com/matijagorsek/genesis.
